# 0069 — Paper ledger reconciliation

Status: accepted for a paper-only Spot ledger. Balances match exactly. A mismatch stays visible and blocks ledger promotion. Live orders stay locked.

## Context

TASK 14.C.02 asks to reconcile order events, fills, positions, fees, and realized PnL with decimal-safe arithmetic, and to add a discrepancy report and a recovery path. Ledger fixtures must balance exactly within documented rounding. A discrepancy is visible and blocks promotion.

Design page 7 says paper mode reconciles positions and cash from append-only ledger events, replays those events after a restart, and raises a reconciliation alert on any mismatch. It says a paper fill is not a live execution and says never to overwrite past fills. Design page 11 names `paper_ledger_event` rows for cash, position, fill, fee, funding, and reconciliation, and it requires decimal-safe values. Design page 16 says the paper ledger is append-only, replayable, and reconciled, and that fees, funding, spread, and slippage are included. The source names no rounding scale. Design page 7 also names 28 qualified paper days before promotion, and design page 2 names two-person approval before a live order path. Decision 0066 charges one fee rate times the filled notional. Decision 0064 stores no fill quantity on the order event. Decision 0068 keeps fault-injection position null.

## Decision

`services/paper-ledger.mjs` exports `createPaperLedgerStore`, `appendPaperLedger`, `reconcilePaperLedger`, `recoverPaperLedger`, `PAPER_LEDGER_KINDS`, `PAPER_LEDGER_ASSUMPTIONS`, and `PAPER_LEDGER_LIMITATIONS`.

The row kinds are `cash`, `position`, `fill`, `fee`, `funding`, and `reconciliation`. A fill stores the paper-fill levels, the fee rate, and the stated fee. The computed fee is the fee rate times the sum of price times quantity. The stated fee must equal that computed fee exactly. A buy increases position and decreases cash by notional plus the computed fee. A sell decreases position and increases cash by notional minus the computed fee. An external cash row changes cash and is not realized PnL. A funding amount is a caller decimal added to cash and to trading cash. The funding interval is NOT IN SOURCE. A position row is a checkpoint and does not move inventory. Spread and slippage stay inside the fill price. No second cash charge is added for them.

Realized PnL is trading cash only when the ledger has at least one fill and the derived position is exactly zero. An open position leaves realized PnL null. Unrealized PnL and average-cost lots are NOT IN SOURCE. The order event must already contain `fill` or `partial fill`. This module reads that event through `readPaperOrder` and does not append one. Order fill quantity remains NOT IN SOURCE. The same idempotency key and body replay the stored row. A different body is `idempotency key is already recorded`.

Equality is exact because the rounding scale is NOT IN SOURCE. A truncated cash or PnL value, a fee that differs from the computed fee, a position checkpoint that differs, or a fill whose order has not reached `fill` or `partial fill` is a discrepancy. The report keeps the derived value and the stated value, sets `alert` true, and sets `promoted` false. `blocked` is `BLOCKED`. Recovery appends one `reconciliation` row and does not change a fill, a fee, or the derived cash. Replaying that recovery key does not append another row. A later paper-order `fill` can clear an order-event discrepancy without editing the ledger row. Ledger promotion does not apply the 28-day gate or two-person approval, and it does not submit an order. `venueClient` stays null. `liveOrderSubmitted` stays false. `liveTrading` stays `OFF`. `liveOrdersLocked` stays true. No PostgreSQL table was added.

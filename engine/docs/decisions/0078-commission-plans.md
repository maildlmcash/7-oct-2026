# 0078 — Commission basis and caps

Status: accepted for versioned commission plan fields and validation. Rates stay caller-supplied basis points. This module does not calculate an accrual or append a ledger entry.

## Context

TASK 17.B.01 asks for product/action, eligible role, approved basis, rate cap, effective dates, currency, hold, refund/reversal, and required approvers. Percentages must not be hard-coded. Validation must reject an out-of-range rate, an overlapping invalid period, and an unauthorized plan edit.

Design section 12 says a child cannot exceed a parent commission ceiling. Super Admin sets global ceilings and approves white-label, distributor, retailer, and API plans. Admin sets a child plan inside the Admin margin and cannot approve that privileged change. Distributor, Retailer, and Customer do not set commission. Customer downstream commission is zero unless it is a separately named product fee, and this module does not rename a fee as commission. Approved bases are the named request, subscription, spread-share, realized-performance, and API metering bases. Unrealized gains, deposits, notional volume, and paper profit are not bases. Parent share plus child share plus platform share cannot exceed the whole eligible pool. Rates are stored in basis points. A rate change applies at a recorded effective time and is not retroactive. Decision 0076 does not delegate a second Super Admin. Decision 0077 does not record a commission field.

## Decision

`services/commission-plans.mjs` exports `createCommissionStore`, `defineCommissionPlan`, `approveCommissionPlan`, and `COMMISSION_PLAN_LIMITATIONS`.

A plan records the product, action, eligible role, basis, rate, rate cap, three pool shares, effective interval, currency, hold, refund, reversal, maker, and required approvers. The tenant tree stays in memory because the tenant table has no parent column. No commission rate literal is stored in the module. A missing rate stays `rate is not configured` and is not filled with zero. The only numeric bound is the design pool, `10000` basis points. A larger rate, cap, or share is `rate is out of range`. A rate above its cap is `rate exceeds cap`. A share total above the pool is `allocation exceeds pool`.

Customer product rate, cap, and child share must be the supplied value zero. Any other supplied value is `customer commission is not allowed`. A basis outside the named set is `basis is not approved`. An action naming unrealized gain, deposits, notional, paper profit, or an unverified signal is `action is not approved`.

Super Admin may define any named product. Admin may define distributor, retailer, and customer plans only inside the Admin subtree, and only under an approved ancestor ceiling that covers the effective start. A missing ceiling is `ceiling is not configured`. A higher child cap is `child exceeds parent ceiling`. Distributor, Retailer, Customer, and Super Distributor edits are `role scope denied` and are not stored. API and white-label products are plan names, not catalog roles.

The effective interval is a fixed UTC timestamp pair. The end is exclusive, so the next plan may start at the previous end. An overlap is `period overlaps an active plan` and the earlier row stays unchanged. An effective start before `changedAt` is `rate change is retroactive`. Hold is a boolean. No hold duration is accepted. Refund and reversal are `reversed` or `adjusted`.

Required approvers are a caller-supplied list of distinct Super Admin ids. An empty list is `approver is not configured`. The maker is `maker cannot approve`. The plan stays `pending` until every listed approver records an approval. This module does not grant the Super Admin role. A credential or bearer value is not stored. `liveTrading` stays `OFF` and `liveOrdersLocked` stays true. No PostgreSQL table was added. `packages/contracts/src/roles.mjs`, `services/role-delegation.mjs`, `services/role-profiles.mjs`, and `services/shell-capabilities.mjs` were not changed.

## Evidence

`pnpm test:commission-plans` passed 3/3, duration_ms 154.31967. Customer, Retailer, Distributor, and Super Distributor cannot define a plan. Admin cannot define an API plan, an admin-share plan, or a plan in `tenant-b`. Admin cannot define a distributor plan before a ceiling exists. Super Admin records `fixture-ceiling` with caller-supplied rate `1000`, cap `4000`, and shares `5000`, `4000`, and `1000`. Those figures are fixtures. The maker cannot approve it. `fixture-super-2` approves it. Rate `4001` is `rate exceeds cap`. Shares totaling `11000` are `allocation exceeds pool`. Cap `10001` and rate `-1` are `rate is out of range` and are not stored. A missing rate is `rate is not configured`. A child cap `4001` is `child exceeds parent ceiling`. Basis `notional volume` and basis `unrealized` are `basis is not approved`. Action `paper profit` is `action is not approved`. A customer rate `1` is `customer commission is not allowed`. A supplied customer rate `0` is stored. Admin records `fixture-child` at rate `500` under the ceiling. Admin cannot approve it. The listed Super Admin can.

`fixture-period` from `2026-10-08T00:00:00Z` to `2026-10-15T00:00:00Z` replays once. An overlapping later plan is `period overlaps an active plan`, and the stored rate stays `1000`. The same plan id with rate `900` does not replace it. A start before `changedAt` is `rate change is retroactive`. A zero-length period is `period is not valid`. The next plan may start at `2026-10-15T00:00:00Z`. A plan that names two approvers stays pending after the first approval and becomes approved only after the second. An unlisted Super Admin is `approver is not recorded`. Hold text and a percent field are rejected. `bearer fixture-token` is not stored. The module source contains no percent sign used as a rate and no hard-coded rate assignment. `pnpm health` returned `{"status":"ok","liveTrading":"OFF","liveOrdersLocked":true}`. Hold duration, Super Distributor commission, accrual amounts, ancestor-cap calculation, and ledger entries are not in this module. Decision 0079 records commission calculation from approved snapshots and does not change these plan rules.
